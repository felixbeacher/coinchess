The house rules

Heads → make a move.
Choose a piece, then a highlighted square.

Tails → miss your turn.
The other player gets the coin.

In check? No coin needed.
You must make a legal move to escape check.

Standard chess moves apply, including castling, en passant and promotion. Checkmate wins; stalemate and bare kings draw. An en passant opportunity expires at the end of the next turn, even on tails.
